Research

01

Vision-language inspection

Weld-PICA

Uncertainty-Aware Vision-Language Weld Inspection

Developed a teacher–student VLM system for weld inspection with defect classification, explanations, and uncertainty estimation. Evaluated reliability using calibration, false accept/reject rates, and human review for resource-constrained deployment.

↗

02

Systems evaluation

CROSS-VLR

System-Level Evaluation of Vision-Language Robotics

Developed a systems-engineering evaluation framework for vision-language robotics, adapting HFACS-style failure analysis to decompose performance across perception, reasoning, and action. Applied it across assistive navigation, industrial inspection, and human–robot interaction.

↗

03

Assistive navigation

Seeing Eye Stretch

Robot-assisted Indoor Navigation for Blind or Low-Vision Individuals

Developed a Hello Robot Stretch RE2 assistive navigation system for blind and low-vision users, integrating vision, LiDAR/infrared navigation, and multimodal VQA for environmental understanding and user interaction.
